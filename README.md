# Toy Forklift Reverse Engineering


A complete multidisciplinary reverse-engineering, CAD modeling, and kinematic simulation of a mechanical toy forklift, developed as part of the ENGG*2100 course at the University of Guelph.


## Project Overview
This repository documents the systematic physical teardown, measurement, and digital reconstruction of a commercial toy forklift kit. Moving from physical components to parametric 3D models and technical 2D drawings, the project evaluates mechanical advantage, gear train kinematics, material selection, and critical mating tolerances.

## Key Mechanical & Design Features
- Mast & Carriage Assembly: Vertical track channels and sliding fork carriage with calculated clearance fits.
- Gear Train Kinematics:Multi-stage spur gear layout analyzing mechanical advantage and speed ratios.
- Chassis & Steering Linkage: Integrated rear-wheel steering geometry and structural ribbing.
- Fit & Tolerancing Analysis: Application of tight tolerances on interacting hole/shaft features (static press-fits and dynamic rotating joints) and loose tolerances on non-critical aesthetic surfaces.

## Software & Engineering Standards
- CAD & Simulation:SolidWorks (Part modeling, Assembly mates, Motion Analysis, and Exploded Views)
- Drafting Standards: ISO A4 portrait sheets, third-angle projection, standardized title blocks, and geometric dimensioning & tolerancing 
- Prototyping & Fabrication: FDM 3D printing feasibility checks



## Repository Structure
- 2D_Drawings: Individual 3D/2D engineering drawings with third-angle projection (.pdf)
- 3D_Solidworks_Files: Individual SolidWorks part files (.SLDPRT) 
- Final_Assembly: Assembly/Exploded view animations and motion study renders (.mp4)
